Haircare Guide

How to use, gently

The product is only half the story. Here's how to work with it.

Wash

Cleanse with lukewarm water and massage the scalp with your fingertips — not nails. Let your shampoo sit, then rinse fully.

Treat

Apply treatments while hair is damp and detangled. Work through mid-lengths to ends, where hair is driest and most fragile.

Seal

Lock in hydration with a light oil. A little goes a long way — warm it between your palms and press into the hair.

Protect

Sleep on a satin or silk pillowcase to reduce friction, breakage and overnight moisture loss.

Haircare Nuggets

Small notes for better hair

Detangle from ends to roots. Start at the bottom and work upward to reduce breakage.

Water is moisturiser. If hair is dry, it first needs water — then an oil to seal it in.

Consistency beats intensity. A simple routine kept up beats a complex one abandoned.

Trim the dead weight. Removing split ends helps the rest of your hair thrive.

Protect from friction. Satin, loose styles and mindful handling protect your edges.

Scalp first. Healthy hair grows from a cared-for scalp. Don't skip it.
