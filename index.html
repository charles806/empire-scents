Our Story

Built By Enyinna Akudo Treasure

AkuSparkle was born when our founder, a hairstylist, noticed that behind many beautiful hairstyles were clients struggling with hair breakage, thinning and other hair concerns. Their struggles sparked a desire to find answers and do more than just style hair.

Through research, learning and a passion for healthier hair, AkuSparkle came to life. Since 2025, we've supported over 100 people with haircare guidance and solutions.

Haircare Nuggets

Small notes for better hair

Detangle from ends to roots. Start at the bottom and work upward to reduce breakage.

Water is moisturiser. If hair is dry, it first needs water — then an oil to seal it in.

Consistency beats intensity. A simple routine kept up beats a complex one abandoned.
