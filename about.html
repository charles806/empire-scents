Our Story

Built By Enyinna Akudo Treasure

AkuSparkle was born when our founder, a hairstylist, noticed that behind many beautiful hairstyles were clients struggling with hair breakage, thinning and other hair concerns. Their struggles sparked a desire to find answers and do more than just style hair.

Through research, learning and a passion for healthier hair, AkuSparkle came to life. Since 2025, we've supported over 100 people with haircare guidance and solutions.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every product we make

Attention

We treat every strand like it matters — because it does. Products and guidance built around understanding, not guessing.

Honesty

No miracle promises, no exaggerated transformations. Just honest products and clear guidance you can trust.

Consistency

Good haircare is a rhythm, not a one-off. We build products that are easy to keep up — consistently.

Natural Care

Thoughtful formulations with natural, effective ingredients — because your scalp deserves care, not harsh stripping.
