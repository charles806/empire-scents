Our Story

The story of AkuSparkle, and why thoughtful haircare matters to us

How it began

The birth of AkuSparkle

AkuSparkle started with a simple frustration: haircare that promised everything but explained nothing. Gift Egwim spent years cycling through products that over-promised and under-delivered — and watching her hair pay for it.

So she started paying closer attention. Reading her own strands. Noting how they responded to water, to oils, to different types of care. She began making products for herself — simple, thoughtful formulations for exactly what her hair needed. Friends noticed, asked, and AkuSparkle was born.

"Sparkle" isn't about shine that fades by evening. It's the way healthy, well-cared-for hair reflects your attention — that quiet confidence that comes from understanding your own curls, coils or strands and showing up for them consistently.
